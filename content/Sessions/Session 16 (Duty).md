*The weight of a nation is unbearable if only one lifts it.*

10/13 - 10/21 502 LM

[[Nabon Trebytheus|Nabon]] and [[Fir (Scion)|Fir]] trek through Castle Giskenroc. The two of them prepare for their upcoming tribunal meeting regarding the circumstances of [[Seraphina (Biome Researcher)|Seraphina]]. Nabon instructs Fir to obtain a reading on the members of the council, since this is one of the few rare opportunities where they'll be in the same room as all of the government elites. Nabon has high expectations for Fir, which Fir self-doubts. Before the pair walks into the room, Nabon adorns a golden, silk blindfold. They deliberate giving one to Fir before ultimately deciding against it. Fir questions the blindfold's purpose, but the tribunal begins and interrupts the question.

Seraphina stands in the middle of two rows of elites, each having an aide by their side, with the young [[King|King Desolpe]] sitting on a half-cut throne hovering above everyone. Everyone in this room other than Seraphina and Fir have the same blindfolds on. Questioning begins. Seraphina explains the assassination attempt made on her life and her exotic encounter in the Petrified Metropolis. While she explains her occurrences, a mystical, unworldly chime echoes in the background of the throne room. These two events are of interest to the council. Firstly, King Desolpe asks if Seraphina wishes to be the head of a new committee in charge of dealing with foreign civilizations, which she accepts on a different day. Nabon instructs Fir to do her best to join the committee. Secondly, King Desolpe asks one of the members and their aide, Augustine and [[Marques]], to come to the front of the room. They fit the description of Seraphina's assassin. Nabon reveals damning evidence, retrieved by his team in previous sessions, to prove that these two were discretely attempting to end Seraphina's biome exploration fanaticism. Marques, the aide, is sentenced to imprisonment for centuries. Augustine, the head elder, is sentenced to a different fate. He takes full responsibility for his actions knowing his gambit has failed, and removes his blindfold to look at King Desolpe. Seraphina asks him why he would do such a thing. Augustine tells her he hopes she may uncover the secrets of the biomes, before disappearing with a chime.

Fast forward a week later, Seraphina, Fir, and a third elf, [[Opal]], sit in a room preparing to meet interviewees for their committee, the Terran Coalition. [[Pop o' Drop]] enters to interview, and seems fairly inept. Their main skill is creating maps. Opal votes in favor of accepting him in. It becomes clear to the other two that she wishes to fill the coalition with useless members, and she wishes to prevent any meaningful progress they may make. After some deliberation, they decide to include him. The next person to interview is [[Shen Wiel]], which causes some alarm for Fir. He's a powerful, respectable candidate, and would be an excellent addition to the team. This immediately calls for suspicion. Opal votes no, ultimately deciding that his stature is too well-received even if he is hiding his true intention for joining. Seraphina and Fir agree, thinking him to be a potentially powerful ally. They vote yes. Rue returns and is immediately accepted as Opal thinks he's useless, Seraphina knows his capabilities, and Fir is too nice to reject anyone. Marques is the fourth interviewer. He's very listless and hard to read. He wishes to join the committee so he doesn't have to go to jail. A lengthy debate goes by before the trio decide to invite him in. [[Saintess Lucille du Promade III|Lucille]] and [[Saintess Lucantor du Promade III|Lucantor]] stop by wishing to sign up, but are unable to join for political reasons. They tell Seraphina she can ask them for favors if she ever needs. Some more unnoteworthy interviews occur afterward.

Seraphina has one task to complete before she can decree her own goals: translating the foreign document she transcribed in the [[Petrified Metropolis]]. She must descend down to a secret, [[Arcane Vault]] underneath the castle that opens once a month to answer difficult questions regarding biomes. Her father was last seen here before disappearing. The party may bring one person from the coalition. They choose to bring Lucille, as she is the only one proficient in the arcane arts out of everyone they interviewed. Upon entering the vault, she receives four mystical motes she can impart into whatever purpose she desires, granted she passes the trials of the vault. She is forced into a conundrum of deciding whether to use these motes on translating the document or finding her father. The first two motes go to trials for the document. They are strenuous battles of wit against constellation elves. The vault gleams that the vault is a conversation between two parties in the foreign civilization.

They are surprised to learn Giskenroc exists in a section titled "The Forgotten Layers," and that the residents are so weak. The first trial highlighted their intention to visit Giskenroc. The second trial revealed they wish to conquer and plunder the lands. One more trial is necessary to find out when they are coming. Seraphina uses one mote to try to learn more information about her father. With a failed arcana check, the mote doesn't stray off its intended purpose well. She knows he was looking for information about their mother, and that is all. She deliberates over what to do with the last mote for a lengthy amount of time. Knowing the invasion date is a powerful piece of information. Lucille tells her she knows what she must do. The last trial is the most difficult of them all, and three members end up dying. Fortunately for them, Lucille is just proficient enough to cast Revivify three times, although this completely expends her. The invasion date is calculated to be in 30 months. The session, and first act, ends here.
